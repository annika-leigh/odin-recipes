S'mores Stuffed Chocolate Chip Cookies

Description

These s'mores stuffed cookies are irresistible chocolate chip cookies to begin with, but then, ooh là là! A surprise center of chocolate, graham cracker, and marshmallow turns them into the perfect mash-up of two favorite treats in one bite.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
  • 2 1/2 cups all purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking poweder
  • 1/2 teaspoon cornstarch
  • 3/4 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips
  • 9 graham cracker sheet halves (2 small rectangles each)
  • 1 (4.4-ounce) bar milk chocolate, cut into 9 even pieces
  • 9 regular marshmallows, cut in half crosswise

Steps

  1. Line two large baking sheets with parchment paper.
  2. Beat butter, brown sugar, and white sugar in the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment on medium-high speed until light and fluffy, about 2 minutes. Add eggs and vanilla; beat until well combined.
  3. Whisk together flour, baking soda, baking powder, cornstarch, and salt in a separate bowl. Gradually add to butter mixture, beating just until combined. Stir in chocolate chips.
  4. Pat dough into a rectangle and cut into 18 portions (6x3 cut) or scoop and level dough into portions using a #16 cookie scoop (or 1/4 cup measuring cup). Place on prepared baking sheet. Chill for 30 minutes.
  5. Flatten each portion of dough into a 3-inch round. Place a graham cracker square in the center of the dough portions, then top with a piece of chocolate and 2 marshmallow halves. Top with another flattened dough round and pinch edges to seal completely.
  6. Arrange cookies on prepared baking sheets, spacing at least 3 inches apart. Freeze for 15 minutes.
  7. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C).
  8. Bake cookies in the preheated oven until golden brown, 13-15 minutes. Let cool on baking sheet for 2 minutes. Carefully transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
