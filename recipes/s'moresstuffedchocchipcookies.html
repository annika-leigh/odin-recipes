S'mores Stuffed Chocolate Chip Cookies

Description

These s'mores stuffed cookies are irresistible chocolate chip cookies to begin with, but then, ooh là là! A surprise center of chocolate, graham cracker, and marshmallow turns them into the perfect mash-up of two favorite treats in one bite.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
  • 2 1/2 cups all purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking poweder
  • 1/2 teaspoon cornstarch
  • 3/4 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips
  • 9 graham cracker sheet halves (2 small rectangles each)
  • 1 (4.4-ounce) bar milk chocolate, cut into 9 even pieces
  • 9 regular marshmallows, cut in half crosswise
