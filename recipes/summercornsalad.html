Summer Corn Salad

Description

This corn salad made with fresh corn kernels and juicy tomatoes tossed in homemade basil vinaigrette is a colorful and deliciously easy side dish you'll want to make all summer!

Ingredients

  • 6 ears corn, husked and cleaned
  • 3 large tomatoes, diced
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh basil
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons white vinegar
  • salt and pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Gather the ingredients
  2. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Cook corn in boiling water for 7 to 10 minutes, or until desired tenderness. Drain, cool, and cut kernels off the cob with a sharp knife.
  3. Combine corn, tomatoes, onion, and basil in a large bowl. Whisk together olive oil and vinegar; season with salt and pepper. Pour vinaigrette over corn mixture and toss to coat. Cover and refrigerate until ready to serve.
